Checking a page

clickpick a line; drag it to move it whole; drag a point to move the point
double-clickon the picked line: a new point there
right-clickon a point of the picked line: take the point away
Shift / Ctrl-clicka line into or out of the chosen ones; dragging one moves them all
Shift-dragon empty ground: choose every line lying wholly inside the box
Ctrl+Achoose every line
Ctrl+C / Xcopy / cut the chosen lines; what is copied stays from page to page
Ctrl+Vpaste them, their top left corner at the pointer; Ctrl+Shift+V where they were
X / Deletetake the chosen lines (or the box) away
Ndraw a line: click along it from either end, Enter or double-click to end, Esc to give up
Jjoin the picked line to the one clicked next
Scut the picked line where the pointer is
Rturn the picked line round: the dot is where it starts — the left end, or the top of a line that runs down the page
Udrag a box over ground nobody vouches for
Zundo
Hhide the lines while held down — is there ink under them?
wheel / dragcloser and farther / move the page; F fits it
Enterthe page is right: keep it and go to the next unchecked
Kset the page aside: blank, a picture, unreadable
